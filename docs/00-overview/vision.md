## Vision Statement

Every online shop deserves one place to sell, track, and run the business — across website, Telegram, Bale, and later Instagram — without juggling five tools and losing orders in the gaps.

DeloRey will become the unified commerce platform Iranian merchants install as their storefront and operations hub. Optional AI Employees sit on top of that same catalog and order truth when merchants want automation — not instead of a coherent shop.

---

## Mission Statement

We give online businesses a **native storefront and one Workspace** so every sale — from the site or messaging channels — is manageable in one product. When a merchant needs it, we add an AI Sales Employee grounded in that same commerce data.

Every day we deepen catalog, order, channel, and ops integration so shop managers stay in control and stop context-switching. AI is an offer inside the platform, not the only reason the product exists.

---

## The Problem We Solve

### Current situation

Most online businesses run on a fragmented stack: a site builder here, Instagram DMs there, Telegram and Bale bots elsewhere, spreadsheets for exceptions. Product knowledge lives in listings and the founder's head. Orders and conversations do not meet in one system. Merchants lose sales to silence and lose sanity to tool sprawl.

### Customer communication problems

Shoppers expect answers and checkout paths wherever they already are — website, Telegram, Bale, Instagram. Humans cannot cover every channel 24/7. Generic chatbots scale without catalog truth and damage trust. The deeper failure is **fragmentation**: the manager cannot see one coherent picture of what sold where.

### Lost sales opportunities

Carts and chat threads die when questions go unanswered or when the buyer must jump tools to complete a purchase. Revenue is lost to latency and to **broken handoffs between channels**, not only to competitors.

### Operational challenges

Small teams cannot staff one person per channel. Copy-paste between tools is the default. Founders become the bottleneck. Existing tools solve pieces — site here, inbox there — but few treat **storefront + multi-channel sales + order ops** as one product.

---

## Our Belief

**Unification beats another point tool.**

The shop manager should not wonder which app holds the order, the stock, or the conversation. One catalog, one order book, one Workspace.

**Channels are surfaces; commerce truth is central.**

Website, Telegram, Bale, and Instagram are how buyers arrive. The product of record is inventory, price, policy, and orders.

**AI should amplify, not redefine, the shop.**

AI Employees extend reach and coverage on top of real commerce data. They are optional. Merchants who only need a solid storefront and order hub still get a complete product.

**Merchants stay in control.**

When AI is enabled, guardrails, human handoff, and audit remain mandatory. Autonomy is earned through accuracy.

## Product Philosophy

### Commerce platform first

The primary product is the **native storefront (سایت‌ساز) + unified ops**. AI is an add-on layer on the same stack. See [Product Positioning](./product-positioning.md).

### Context over chat theater

Any AI reply or sales assist must use live catalog, inventory, orders, and policies.

### Revenue and clarity focused

Success is measurable sales completed and a manager who is not confused — not message volume alone.

### One brain for channels

Channel adapters stay thin. Business rules live once in Commerce Core and Workspace.

### Humans in the loop

Escalation and operator takeover are product features whenever AI is on.

---

## Long-term direction

1. Deepen the native storefront and CMS until it is the default home for SMB online shops in the beachhead.
2. Unify order capture and tracking across website and messaging channels.
3. Offer AI Sales (then Support) Employees as clear, optional packages on that foundation.
4. Expand channels and connectors without splitting the merchant’s source of truth.

Architecture for the AI Runtime remains important for the add-on path; it does not redefine company identity as “AI-only SaaS.”
